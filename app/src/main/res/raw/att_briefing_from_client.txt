CLIENT BRIEFING: SERVICE PAGE WEBSITE

Prepared for the design team
Revision 2

1. GOAL
Build a single service page that explains what the company does and
converts visitors into enquiries. The page replaces three older pages
that currently split the same message.

2. AUDIENCE
Operations managers at mid-sized firms. They skim first and read
second, so the first screen must carry the whole offer.

3. BRAND DIRECTION
Primary blue and a warm red accent. Plenty of white space. No stock
photography of people shaking hands.

4. SECTIONS REQUIRED
   - Hero with headline, one line of support copy and a single button
   - Three service cards, each with a price indication
   - Short proof section with two client quotes
   - Contact form with name, company, email and message

5. CONSTRAINTS
   Launch in six weeks. Copy will arrive in week three. Build the
   layout with placeholder copy until then.

6. OPEN QUESTIONS
   Do we need a pricing page as well, or do the cards cover it?
   Who signs off the final copy?
